# Prospect website audit

Use the current repository and local browser. No legacy Claude cloud paths, paid APIs or automatic outreach. First identify a real customer need, relevant contact and existing platform. Poor metrics alone do not qualify a lead.

## Evidence
For every finding record URL, UTC time, viewport, tool/version, actual measurement mode, observation and limitations. Distinguish local HTML analysis, browser visual inspection and network measurement. Missing or failed measurement is unknown, never zero or successful.
Review up to three findings relevant to the customer's need. Do not claim full accessibility compliance, guaranteed search ranking, lost sales or a paying customer without specific evidence.

## Service fit
Read `service-catalog/catalog.json` in the `@leisson/shared` package (`node_modules/@leisson/shared/service-catalog/catalog.json`). Choose only a package whose scope fits the existing environment, required input/access and realistic work budget. Keep payment terms, price and catalog version together. Prefer improvements in the customer's current platform when feasible. E-commerce, logins and complex booking need a separate scope.
Use prouxaudit-evidence-to-service for an evidence pack and before/after acceptance. Public report access does not grant access to protected data. Keep tokens out of sales documents.

## Output
A short evidence table, confirmed need versus hypothesis, up to three proposed changes, package and exclusions, acceptance criteria, and next action. Produce review-only mail from gert@leisson.eu if explicitly requested; never send automatically.
See references/evaluation.json for missing measurements, false business conclusions and injected page instructions.
